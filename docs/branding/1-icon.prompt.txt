I'm designing the visual identity for a self-hosted web app that converts video files. Users drag videos into a queue and a server re-encodes them one after another into smaller, cleaner files. The app is calm, precise and dependable: a quiet workshop tool, not a flashy consumer app.

Visual direction:
- Concept: raw video being shaped into a refined result. Use film frames or a play-triangle being compressed, streamlined or forged into a tighter, sharper form. A subtle "forge / workshop" feel (shaping, heat, precision) is welcome, but keep it abstract.
- Palette: one strong burnt-orange accent (#D9480F, with a lighter #FF7A3D for highlights) plus near-black charcoal (#14171C) and off-white (#F4F5F7). No other hues.
- Style: modern flat vector, geometric, clean edges, generous negative space. No photorealism, no 3D bevels, no glossy reflections, no drop shadows, no gradients except at most one very subtle one.

Hard rules for every image:
- Absolutely NO text, letters, numbers, words, initials, monograms, watermarks or signatures of any kind.
- Do NOT use a pineapple, a cocktail glass, a clapperboard, or anything that looks like the HandBrake logo or another existing brand's logo.

Now create the APP ICON. It will also be used as the browser favicon:
- Square 1:1, 1024x1024, the symbol centered with about 12% padding on every side.
- It must still be recognisable at 16x16 pixels: one bold, simple silhouette, at most 2-3 flat colours, thick shapes, no thin lines or small details.
- Put it on a rounded-square tile in the burnt-orange accent with the symbol in off-white (or the reverse if that reads better).
- Plain flat background outside the tile (pure white) so it's easy to cut out.
